How it works

01

Start the timer

Press the button, set the time and Lophi starts. Brushing time is on.

What makes Lophi special

A new surprise every single time.

Most timers just count down. Lophi rewards your child at the end with a little animation: a heart, a star, a cat and many more. Never the same twice, so brushing stays exciting day after day.
